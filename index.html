How To Play

Essential Pétanque Rules

Learn the fundamentals of the game in 4 quick steps.

1. The Cochonnet

Draw a throwing circle (35-50 cm). Throw the small wooden target ball (*cochonnet* or jack) 6 to 10 meters away.

2. Feet Anchored

Pétanque comes from *pès tancats* (feet planted on the ground). Stand inside the circle with both feet flat when throwing.

3. Point or Shoot

*Pointers* roll boules closest to the jack. *Shooters* lob boules to knock opponent boules away (*carreau*).

4. Scoring to 13

The team closest to the jack scores 1 point for each boule closer than the opponent's best boule. First to 13 wins!
